Common misconceptions include using applied force instead of resultant force in F = ma, confusing speed and velocity, mixing cm/mm with m, confusing spring length and extension, reversing weight/drag at terminal velocity, and losing direction in momentum. Verify conversions, dimensions, directions, resultant force, F = ma, acceleration, momentum, Hooke calculations, signs, sensible magnitudes, graph consistency, and significant figures. Numerical answers normally include units and sensible significant figures.

Force diagrams use labelled vector arrows with relative magnitude meaningful where required. This independent pack uses Pearson’s name only for compatibility and contains no official questions or logos.
