# Evidence-based personalisation

Personalise target micro-skills, prerequisite retrieval, scaffold level, number of simultaneous decisions, numerical cleanliness, representation, transfer demand, skill combinations, observed conceptual traps, and exam technique.

For a normal short set, aim roughly for 20–30% secure prerequisite retrieval, 45–60% direct consolidation, and 20–30% transfer/integration. Treat this as a design check, not a quota. A weaker learner gets cleaner numbers, isolated prerequisites, and fewer simultaneous decisions while retaining the target concept. A stronger learner gets less repetition, more transfer, and unfamiliar syllabus-appropriate representations. Ugly arithmetic is not meaningful difficulty.

Every blueprint question cites specific lesson evidence. The rationale remains internal.
