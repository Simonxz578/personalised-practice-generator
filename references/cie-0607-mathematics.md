# Cambridge IGCSE International Mathematics 0607 pack

Qualification: Cambridge IGCSE International Mathematics 0607. Routes: Core and Extended where applicable. Pack version 0.2; last verified 2026-08-19. Before high-stakes use, confirm the examination-year syllabus at the [official Cambridge qualification page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-international-mathematics-0607/).

Initial supported topics: percentages; straight lines; coordinate geometry; quadratic equations/functions; algebraic manipulation; statistics; common graph skills.

Use concise British English and command words such as calculate, determine, find, show that, explain, describe, estimate, sketch, construct, factorise, simplify, solve, compare, and justify. Typical marks: 1 for a fact/result, 2–3 for standard working, 4–6 for multistage reasoning. Extended questions may integrate skills; Core must not inherit Extended-only demand.

Common misconceptions include reverse/repeated percentage change, sign loss in algebra, negative-coordinate subtraction, gradient/reciprocal confusion, transformation direction, and statistics denominator/total errors. Verify arithmetic, equations by substitution, factorisations by expansion, coordinates, midpoint/distance/gradient/line equations, intersections, percentages, statistics totals, graph coordinates, exact/decimal conventions, and mark totals. Use exact forms unless approximation is requested; state rounding accuracy.

Diagrams use labelled vector axes, readable scales and “not drawn to scale” where needed. This independent pack uses board names only for compatibility and contains no official questions or logos.
