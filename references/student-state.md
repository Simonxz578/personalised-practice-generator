# Student state interpretation

Preserve teacher observations verbatim where possible. Current-lesson evidence has high weight. Prompted success is not independent mastery; an isolated slip is not automatically a misconception. Repeated related errors may be stored as an error pattern. When cause is unsupported, put it in `unknown_causes` rather than guessing.

The optional internal 0–4 micro-skill scale means: 0 insufficient evidence; 1 introduced but not independently usable; 2 inconsistent; 3 mostly secure independently; 4 secure and transferable. It is a teaching aid, not a measurement, and never appears in student PDFs.

Every micro-skill judgement stores its evidence, independence status, and source. Do not infer unrelated personal attributes. Do not test exclusions or silently expand curriculum scope.
