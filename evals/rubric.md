# Evaluation rubric

Score each category 0–4 separately: A extraction (independent/prompted/unstable preserved; no invention); B personalisation (targets evidence and priorities; secure skills light); C curriculum accuracy; D original, clear, answerable question quality; E answer/units/marks accuracy; F document quality (files, 1/1/2 pages, no clipping, overlap, leakage, or numbering errors).

For representative cases compare without-Skill and with-Skill runs. Record model/name/version/date, input, state, blueprint, paper, validator result, human pedagogical score, and PDF result. Make no model-quality claim without these data. The central metric is how much the Skill reduces the quality gap.
